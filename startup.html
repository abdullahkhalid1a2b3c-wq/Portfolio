Co-Founder & Technical Partner
2026 – Present
🏆 Ranked 1st Nationally at the BanoQabil × Alibaba Cloud AI Hackathon 2026

FasalDoc is an AI-powered crop and livestock disease diagnosis platform created specifically to support smallholder farmers across Pakistan. As a Co-Founder and Technical Partner, I collaborated on the architectural design, AI model integration, and system implementation.

Key Technical Pillars

🌾 Disease Diagnosis Engine

Built a multi-provider LLM diagnosis engine to identify crop illnesses, livestock symptoms, and recommend tailored treatments.

📶 Offline-First Architecture

Engineered an offline-first mobile/web application structure for seamless operation in low-connectivity rural environments.

🇵🇰 Urdu/English Support

Integrated full Urdu and English bilingual language support to lower barriers for rural farmers across Pakistan.

☁️ Cloud & AI Stack

Integrated scalable AI inference endpoints with robust data management and Alibaba Cloud infrastructure.
