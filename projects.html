AI Daily News to Instagram Post Generator

Agentic AI & GenAI
Python LangChain LangGraph Mistral AI FastAPI Streamlit Pillow

Engineered an automated Generative AI pipeline that retrieves real-time news from major RSS feeds including Dawn, BBC, TechCrunch, Sky Sports, and Al Jazeera. The system generates complete 6-slide Instagram carousels, engaging captions, targeted hashtags, and strategic call-to-actions (CTAs) using Mistral AI / ChatMistralAI.

  • LangChain Components: Leveraged RunnableParallel, RunnableBranch, and structured outputs for deterministic JSON generation.
  • LangGraph Workflow: Designed a stateful workflow implementing an evaluator-optimizer pattern.
  • Iterative Refinement: The generated content is automatically scored from 0–10 and iteratively refined against an 8.0 quality threshold with a maximum of 3 iterations.
  • Full-Stack Architecture: Built a high-performance FastAPI backend alongside an interactive Streamlit user interface.
  • Slide Rendering: Utilized Pillow (PIL) to render approved posts into six high-resolution 1080×1350 PNG carousel slides.

AI Video Assistant – RAG-Based Video Agent

RAG & Multimodal AI
Python LangChain Mistral AI Whisper ChromaDB Hugging Face Sentence Transformers Streamlit

Built an end-to-end Generative AI application that ingests YouTube or local video content and provides intelligent conversational question-answering grounded strictly in the video transcript.

  • Audio Transcription: Integrated OpenAI Whisper for local audio extraction and speech-to-text transcription.
  • Vector Storage & Retrieval: Implemented a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline using LangChain and ChromaDB vector store.
  • Semantic Search: Used Hugging Face sentence-transformer embeddings to semantically retrieve relevant transcript chunks matching user queries.
  • Grounded LLM Responses: Grounded Mistral AI model responses in retrieved video contexts through an intuitive Streamlit chat interface.

FasalDoc – AI-Powered Crop & Livestock Diagnosis Platform

Startup & AgriTech AI
🏆 Ranked 1st Nationally at the BanoQabil × Alibaba Cloud AI Hackathon 2026
Multi-Provider LLM Engine Offline-First Architecture Urdu/English Bilingual Full-Stack Development

FasalDoc is an AI-powered crop and livestock disease diagnosis platform built specifically for smallholder farmers across Pakistan. I co-founded FasalDoc and served as a technical partner alongside the founding team.

  • LLM Engine: Designed a multi-provider LLM diagnosis engine capable of identifying crop diseases, symptoms, and treatment options.

  • Bilingual Support: Developed full Urdu and English bilingual language support to maximize farmer usability.
